Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order? ＋

Browse or search for products, add your favourites to the cart, then proceed to checkout. Sign in or create an account to complete your order.

How much is delivery? ＋

Standard delivery is ₦2,500. Delivery is free on orders of ₦100,000 or more. Your delivery fee is shown before you place the order.

How can I track my order? ＋

Use the order tracking section and enter the order number shown after checkout. You can also find your order number in your account's order history.

Which payment methods are available? ＋

This starter version demonstrates checkout without charging money. Paystack or Flutterwave can be connected to accept real card and bank payments.

Can I return an item? ＋

Contact our support team within 7 days of delivery with your order number and details of the item. Perishable goods may have special return conditions.
